About Kip

Meet Kip. Not here to be nice.

Kip is a small, grumpy frog who lives on your phone and has exactly one job: making sure you do what you said you would. No lectures, no locked screens. Just a very pointed comment at the right moment.

The story

Why Kip exists

You open your notes. Then your phone. Forty minutes later you know everything about a stranger's kitchen renovation and nothing about your exam. Kip was built for that moment.

Blockers get bypassed

App limits come with a "remind me later" button. You've pressed it. Everyone has. Restrictions don't change why you reached for the phone.

Willpower runs out

Relying on discipline alone works until you're tired, stressed, or bored. Which is usually right when the studying should happen.

Habits stick

Kip makes you notice the habit while it's happening. Notice it often enough, and you start closing the app before Kip has to say anything.

Good to know

Questions

Everything people ask before letting a frog judge their screen time.

Who is Kip for?

Students and anyone who struggles with procrastination and wants to change their scrolling habits.

Does Kip block my apps?

No. Kip motivates instead of restricting. You pick which apps count as distractions, and Kip calls you out.

What about my data?

App usage tracking is strictly opt-in, with your consent, and used only to power Kip's nudges.

When can I get it?

Kip is in early development. Join the waitlist and you'll be among the first to try it.

Is Kip actually mean?

Kip is passive-aggressive, not cruel. The roasts are about your habits, never about you, and Kip is genuinely happy when you get things done.

How much does Kip cost?

Kip is free to start. Basic and Pro add customization, deeper stats, and conversations with Kip. See pricing
